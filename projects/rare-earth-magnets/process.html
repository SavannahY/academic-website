How to read this animation

The artwork is an AI-generated representative reconstruction. Source links establish process functions and named equipment examples, not the accuracy of every illustrated pipe or component.

Moving machinery layers and changing material shapes explain mechanisms. The tour clock does not encode mass, electricity, throughput, safety procedures or actual cycle time. Cutaways reveal hidden processes; actual operation requires the relevant enclosure to be sealed.

Hard-rock beneficiation is one route. Ion-adsorption clays and alternative separation routes require different upstream equipment. Optional grain-boundary diffusion is shown separately.

Magnetic alignment during pressing and final magnetization are different steps. A machined magnet material does not acquire its intended working magnetization simply because it has a shape.

02 / U.S. capability priorities

Independence means
a qualified part.

These are priorities to investigate by application, not a quantified policy ranking. A mine alone cannot substitute for separation, metalmaking, processing skill and customer qualification.

A design may use different magnet chemistry or avoid rare-earth permanent magnets. Sector labels do not mean that every EV, wind turbine, robot or data center needs NdFeB. Defense and SmCo require a separate application-specific assessment.

Questions to take into a research conversation

For one accepted NdFeB grade and customer region, when does manufacturing relocation turn material availability into usable independent capability?

Compare a common decision date, shock date and delivery deadline. Distinguish construction, ramp and customer qualification before interpreting investment rankings.

Under fixed total mining capacity, when does geographic diversification change delivery, and when do downstream constraints bind?

How do grade qualification, equipment lead times, spare capacity and finite stocks change recovery time?

What measured plant costs would be needed to compare separation, qualified manufacturing and inventory under a real dollar budget?

Does reducing Dy/Tb dependence through grain-boundary diffusion or another qualified design reduce deployment delay, and at what performance or cost trade-off?

Start from a mass-balanced, traceable baseline. The current model is a preliminary extension of material-intensity demand accounting; it does not reproduce Davis’s national trade-risk scenarios.
